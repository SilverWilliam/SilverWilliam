# William Silver

I’m a final-year DPhil student at the University of Oxford researching
neuromodulation and flexible computation in biological and artificial
neural networks.

My work combines behavioural neuroscience experiments (fiber photometry recordings from brains of mice whilst they learn and make flexible decisions), and computational modelling (e.g. fitting RL/Bayesian models to behaviour, training tiny recurrent neural networks under biologically inspired constraints to align behavioural and neuromodulator data).
